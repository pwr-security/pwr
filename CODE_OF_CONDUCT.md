# Code of Conduct

The Physical Weakness Registry is a technical project. We want contributors to work together calmly and respectfully, whatever their background or experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful. Criticize ideas and work, not people.
- Assume good faith, and explain your reasoning.
- Accept decisions on scope and wording once they are made, and stay on topic.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Posting vulnerabilities of a specific real facility, organization or person. This project describes general weaknesses, not targets.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (repository, issues, pull requests, discussions) and to anyone representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content, lock discussions, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, write to [mateo@callec.net](mailto:mateo@callec.net). Reports are handled in confidence.
